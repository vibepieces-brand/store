Jewelry Care Guide

Gold-Plated Pieces

Keep them dry — remove before showering, swimming, or exercising. Store separately in a soft pouch to avoid scratches, and wipe gently with a dry microfiber cloth after each wear. Avoid perfume and lotion contact directly on the plating.

Stainless Steel Pieces

Highly durable and water-resistant, but a soft cloth after wear keeps the shine. Occasional cleaning with mild soap and warm water restores brilliance — avoid abrasive cleaners or chlorine exposure.

General Tips

Store each piece separately, apply cosmetics before jewelry, and give your pieces a rest between wears to extend their lifetime radiance.
